us-military-europe-map

An interactive map of publicly acknowledged U.S. military bases, installations, and rotational deployments across Europe.

This project follows the definition and locations listed in Appendix B of the Congressional Research Service report:https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48123#_Ref157941229, the Büchel Air Base has been added because it has nuclear capacities.
The map includes permanent, semi-permanent, NATO-linked, and rotational U.S. military presence throughout the European continent. It is not a complete or definitive list, but rather a public-reference tool built from open sources.



Some installations do not maintain updated public websites. For official installation information, you can also consult:

Military OneSource – https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/view-all
NATO on the Map - https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/organization/nato-on-the-map
Lists of U.S. Military Bases Abroad, 1776–2021 American University Digital Research Archive https://www.basenation.us/maps.html
